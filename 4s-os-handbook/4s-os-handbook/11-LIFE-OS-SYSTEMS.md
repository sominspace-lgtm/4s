# Life OS Systems

These are long-term product systems that make 4S OS more than a planner.

They should be built gradually, only when they reduce mental load.

## Life Autopilot

Quietly maintain recurring life needs.

Examples:

- Replace HVAC filter.
- Renew passport.
- Refill medication.
- Buy toothpaste.
- Change smoke detector batteries.
- Water plants.
- Car registration.
- Insurance renewal.
- Subscription review.

These appear as gentle maintenance, not alarms.

## Energy Mode

Ask optionally:

“How much energy do you have today?”

- Low
- Normal
- High

Brief adapts.

Low: one task, one habit, one reflection.

High: deeper planning, projects, reviews.

## Recovery Mode

Triggered when user says:

- “I'm overwhelmed.”
- “I'm exhausted.”
- “I'm burned out.”

4S OS simplifies itself.

Only essentials remain.

## Relationship Memory

Help users become more thoughtful.

For each person:

- birthday
- favorite coffee
- gift ideas
- last conversation
- important details
- children/pets/partner names if saved
- topics to ask about

Private by default.

## Decision Memory

Store decisions, not just notes.

Fields:

- decision
- date
- options considered
- reason
- expected outcome
- actual outcome later

Future-you should not repeat the same thinking twice.

## Home Brain

Store household knowledge:

- Wi-Fi
- utilities
- paint colors
- furniture measurements
- appliance manuals
- maintenance schedules
- insurance
- serial numbers
- emergency contacts

## Personal Manual

The user's owner manual.

Includes:

- morning routine
- night routine
- when overwhelmed
- packing list
- medical information
- emergency contacts
- favorite restaurants
- preferred work style

## Life Timeline

Not task history. Life history.

Examples:

- trips
- books finished
- achievements
- jobs
- milestones
- major conversations
- memories
- completed goals

Make life searchable.

## Future Me

Messages to future self.

Deliver:

- in six months
- one year
- birthday
- project completion
- retirement
- 100-day habit

## Friction Detection

Notice repeated patterns:

- postponed tasks
- skipped habits
- ignored domains
- recurring stressful events

Suggest smaller, kinder adjustments.

## Context Awareness

Only with permission.

Examples:

- If raining, suggest indoor workout.
- If near Costco, show shopping list.
- If traveling, show packing checklist.
- If calendar is full, reduce Brief.

## Whispers

Replace notifications with gentle phrasing.

Examples:

- “Whenever you're ready...”
- “Something worth checking today.”
- “This can wait until later.”

## Tiny Rituals

Morning:

- greeting
- today's focus
- one useful note

Evening:

- one win
- one reflection
- tomorrow's first step

Keep both under one minute.

## Wins

Track accomplishments, not just obligations.

Examples:

- maintained streak
- called someone
- finished reading
- reviewed finances
- protected attention
- rested when needed

## Seasons of Life

Let 4S OS adapt to the user's current season:

- Building
- Balancing
- Recovering
- Exploring
- Settling
- Launching

A season can influence Brief, Council, AI tone, suggested habits, review questions, notifications, and default Guide.
