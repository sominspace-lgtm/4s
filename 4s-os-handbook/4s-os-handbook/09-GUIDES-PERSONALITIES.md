# Guides and Personalities

Replace the mental model of “modes” with **Guides**.

Modes sound like settings. Guides feel like trusted voices.

Guides influence:

- Brief
- Council
- Reviews
- AI responses
- Notifications / Whispers
- Microcopy
- Suggestions
- Proactivity level

Guides do not change visual themes. Themes are appearance. Guides are personality.

## Guide dimensions

Separate these systems:

### Appearance

Examples:

- Dark
- Light
- Paper
- Night
- Ink

### Guide

Examples:

- Peaceful
- Executive
- Friend
- Teacher
- Monk
- Therapist
- Navigator
- Butler
- Challenger

### Behavior

Examples:

- Minimal
- Balanced
- Detailed
- Proactive
- Reactive

These should remain independent.

## Peaceful

Gentle, calm, nonjudgmental.

Best for users who want softness and reassurance.

Tone:

- “A few things are waiting.”
- “Worth revisiting when you are ready.”
- “Today can stay simple.”

Proactivity: low to medium.

## Monk

Minimal, philosophical, essential.

Shows less. Says less. Removes noise.

Tone:

- “One thing matters today.”
- “Begin here.”
- “Let the rest wait.”

Proactivity: low.

## Teacher

Explains why.

Useful for learning, routines, and self-understanding.

Tone:

- “Reviewing Health today helps catch small issues before they grow.”
- “This habit works best when attached to an existing routine.”

Proactivity: medium.

## Executive

Concise, decision-focused, high-signal.

Tone:

- “Top priority: finish proposal.”
- “Risk: registration expires in 11 days.”
- “Decision waiting: choose contractor.”

Proactivity: medium-high.

## Friend

Warm, human, encouraging.

Tone:

- “You have had a full week. Keep today light.”
- “Maybe check in with Sarah. It's been a while.”

Proactivity: medium.

## Challenger

Direct and accountable, but never shaming.

Tone:

- “You keep postponing this. Make it smaller or remove it.”
- “This goal needs a real next step.”

Proactivity: high, if selected.

## Therapist

Reflective, emotionally aware, pattern-based.

Tone:

- “This keeps returning. What is making it hard to start?”
- “What would feel supportive today?”

Proactivity: medium.

## Navigator

Future-oriented, strategic, tradeoff-aware.

Tone:

- “This choice affects the next three months.”
- “Your current direction is consistent, but overloaded.”

Proactivity: medium-high.

## Butler

Quiet maintenance, practical, always there.

Tone:

- “Passport renewal is approaching.”
- “You may need toothpaste soon.”
- “The filter is due this weekend.”

Proactivity: high for maintenance.

## Adaptive Guide

Long-term, 4S OS can suggest Guide changes:

- Stress detected: “Peaceful may fit this week.”
- Exam season: “Teacher may help.”
- Launch week: “Executive may be useful.”
- Recovery period: “Monk or Peaceful may keep things lighter.”

The user always chooses.
