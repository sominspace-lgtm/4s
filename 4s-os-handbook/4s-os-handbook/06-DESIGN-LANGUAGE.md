# Design Language

4S OS should feel minimal, warm, journal-like, Muji calm, and premium without feeling cold.

## Visual mood

Use references like:

- warm Apple, not sterile Apple
- Muji simplicity
- a quiet journal
- a calm desk
- a soft personal dashboard
- Things 3 clarity
- Linear precision, softened

## Layout

Use generous spacing.

Prefer fewer objects per view.

Group related items in calm cards or sections.

Avoid dense dashboards.

## Typography

Typography should be readable, soft, and confident.

Use hierarchy through size, weight, spacing, and contrast rather than decoration.

Avoid tiny labels for important information.

## Color

Color should guide, not decorate.

Themes are visual only and should never change information architecture or personality logic.

Accent colors must maintain accessible contrast.

Avoid hardcoded colors that ignore themes.

## Cards

Cards should feel like surfaces, not boxes.

Use:

- soft borders
- subtle shadows when appropriate
- ample internal padding
- clear titles
- calm status labels

## Buttons

Every screen should have one obvious primary action.

Secondary actions should be quieter.

Tertiary actions should often be links.

Do not make unrelated actions equal weight.

## Motion

Motion should feel like breathing.

Use subtle transitions for:

- opening bottom sheets
- changing cards
- expanding forms
- confirming completion

Respect reduced motion.

Avoid flashy entrance animations.

## Icons

Icons should clarify, not decorate.

Use simple, consistent icon style.

Do not rely on icon-only controls unless the meaning is obvious or labeled.

## Finished feeling

A screen feels finished when:

- the hierarchy is obvious
- copy is polished
- loading states are handled
- empty states are intentional
- errors are human-readable
- mobile layout is not cramped
- there are no placeholder-like controls
