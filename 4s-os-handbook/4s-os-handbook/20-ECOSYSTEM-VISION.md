# Ecosystem Vision

4S OS can eventually become an ecosystem, but only if each surface reduces friction.

## Principle

Every new platform must answer:

“What can this surface do better than the main app?”

If the answer is unclear, do not build it.

## Web

The current core product.

Best for:

- planning
- reviewing
- editing
- money hub
- council
- settings

## Mobile

Primary daily surface.

Best for:

- Brief
- Quick Capture
- Whispers
- routines
- relationship nudges
- on-the-go search

## Widgets

Best for:

- today's focus
- next action
- quick capture
- habit rhythm
- one gentle reminder

Widgets must never become dashboards.

## Watch

Best for:

- one-tap capture
- habit confirmation
- gentle whispers
- next calendar item

## Voice

Best for:

- capture without typing
- “what matters today?”
- household maintenance
- quick reminders

## Browser extension

Best for:

- save links
- capture purchases to wishlist/buy-again
- clip decisions or research
- add renewal/subscription clues

## Email integration

Best for:

- renewal detection
- shipping / returns
- receipts
- bills
- events
- important follow-ups

Must be private, opt-in, and transparent.

## Calendar integration

Best for:

- Brief context
- energy planning
- weekly review
- focus protection

## Smart home / Alexa

Best for:

- household capture
- grocery/refill needs
- voice maintenance reminders

## 4S v10 vision

The ideal future version:

- opens with a one-minute Brief
- quietly maintains home, health, money, relationships, routines, and decisions
- adapts to user energy and season of life
- uses local/private AI where possible
- makes important things visible before they become urgent
- lets users export and delete easily
- feels warm, alive, and trustworthy

The aspiration:

In ten years, people say:

“4S OS helped me out so much. It made life feel lighter.”
