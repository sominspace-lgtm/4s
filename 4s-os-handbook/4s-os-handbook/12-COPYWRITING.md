# Copywriting

4S OS should sound like a trusted advisor: warm, calm, clear, and human.

## Avoid

- Optimize
- Crush
- Hustle
- Hack
- Supercharge
- Maximum output
- Unlock your potential
- AI-powered everything
- All-in-one productivity platform

## Prefer

- Nothing urgent.
- You're caught up.
- Worth revisiting.
- Whenever you're ready.
- Protect your attention.
- Today looks calm.
- One calm place for what matters.
- Built for planning, reviewing, and staying aligned.
- Private by default.

## Tone rules

Use short sentences.

Be specific.

Avoid hype.

Avoid guilt.

Avoid corporate SaaS language.

Use warmth without being childish.

## Auth copy

Headline:

4S

Tagline:

Your personal operating system

Supporting copy:

Organize your goals, tasks, routines, reflections, and priorities in one calm workspace.

Alternative:

Build your personal system for planning, reviewing, and staying aligned.

Primary CTA:

Continue with magic link

Secondary:

Sign in with password

Tertiary:

Create an account

Success:

Check your email for a secure sign-in link.

Loading:

Sending magic link...

Checking your account...

Creating your space...

Error:

That email and password did not match. Try again or use a magic link.

Too many attempts. Please wait a moment before trying again.

Queue clear. Protect your attention.

Build your first ritual.

## Progress copy

- Reviewed.
- Ready.
- Nice rhythm.
- You protected your attention.
- This week is taking shape.
- One thing at a time.

## Whispers

- Whenever you're ready...
- Worth checking today.
- This can wait.
- A small thing is waiting.
- You may want to revisit this.

## Daily reflection questions

Use one question only:

- What are you avoiding?
- Who deserves your attention?
- What mattered today?
- What feels lighter than yesterday?
- What deserves tomorrow?
- What would make today meaningful?
