# Council

Council is a signature experience of 4S OS.

Council is not AI chat.

Council is a personal board of advisors for life.

Each advisor should have a clear mission, tone, and reason for speaking.

## Council purpose

Council helps the user zoom out.

It asks:

- What area of life is being neglected?
- What deserves attention before it becomes urgent?
- What pattern is forming?
- What needs reflection, not just action?

## Advisors

### Finance Advisor

Mission: help the user stay financially aware without anxiety.

Focus:

- renewals
- recurring purchases
- wishlists
- budgets
- subscriptions
- upcoming financial decisions

Tone: grounded, practical, calm.

### Health Advisor

Mission: support consistency, recovery, and basic wellbeing.

Focus:

- routines
- appointments
- medication/refills if applicable
- movement
- sleep
- energy

Tone: caring, nonjudgmental.

### Relationship Advisor

Mission: help the user become more thoughtful with people.

Focus:

- last contact
- birthdays
- gift ideas
- meaningful conversations
- people who deserve attention

Tone: warm, human.

### Business / Work Advisor

Mission: keep work goals from drifting.

Focus:

- projects
- decisions
- follow-ups
- ignored areas
- risks

Tone: clear and direct.

### Creative Advisor

Mission: protect creative momentum.

Focus:

- ideas
- drafts
- projects
- inspiration
- unfinished creative threads

Tone: encouraging, spacious.

### Home Advisor

Mission: reduce household friction.

Focus:

- maintenance
- bills
- supplies
- repairs
- documents
- recurring chores

Tone: practical and quiet.

### Planning Advisor

Mission: help the user choose direction.

Focus:

- weekly review
- priorities
- upcoming events
- timelines
- decisions

Tone: strategic and concise.

### Self Advisor

Mission: help the user stay aligned with themselves.

Focus:

- reflection
- values
- energy
- season of life
- goals
- identity

Tone: gentle, wise.

## Council writing rule

Advisors explain why something matters.

Bad:

“Business has not been reviewed.”

Good:

“Business has been quiet for 12 days. A short review now can catch small drift before it becomes pressure.”

## Mobile Council

On mobile, Council should be card-by-card, not a dense grid.

Swipe between advisors.

Each card should have:

- status
- one insight
- one suggested action
- dismiss/snooze

## Council should not nag

Council speaks when useful.

Silence is allowed.

“Nothing needs attention” is a valid advisor state.
